TGS Tournament Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains what information TGS Tournament Manager may process to provide its features.

Information We May Process

This may include account name and email address, tournament information, player names, fixtures, scores, reports, and technical information needed to operate the service.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide features, authenticate users, save tournament records, maintain the service, troubleshoot problems, and improve the application.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to host, store, secure, or operate the application.

Public Tournament Information

Tournament and player information may be shared through features that users choose to publish or share.

Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the TGS project owner.
